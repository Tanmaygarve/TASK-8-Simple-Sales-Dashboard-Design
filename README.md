# 🧮 Task 8 - Simple Sales Dashboard Design (Data Analyst Internship)

## 📌 Objective:
Design a clean and interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze sales performance by product, region, and category.

## 🛠 Tools Used:
- Power BI
- Dataset used: Sales Order data (custom dataset)
- (Optional cleaning/preparation done in Excel)

## 📊 Dashboard Features:
- **Top 5 States by Profit** – Bar chart highlighting leading states
- **Sum of Amount by Category** – Category-wise sales performance
- **Sum of Amount by Payment Mode** – Donut chart visualizing customer payment preferences
- **Sub-category Wise Breakdown** – Sales and profit comparison
- **Customer Insights** – Top 5 customers based on sales
- **Interactive Filters** – By City and Category
- **Map View** – City-wise profit visualization

## 🔍 Key Insights:
1. **Madhya Pradesh** and **Maharashtra** lead in profits with ₹7.4K and ₹7.0K respectively.
2. **Electronics** is the highest-grossing category with ₹166K in total sales.
3. **COD (Cash on Delivery)** is the most preferred payment mode (35.45% of transactions).
4. Despite strong sales, **Phones** and **Electronic Games** show minimal or negative profits, indicating margin issues.
